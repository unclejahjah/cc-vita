# Relics / Key Items

Source: cc-resource `Levels/Levels.md`, `Levels/Dock.md`, `Gameplay/Items.md`. Note: `Gameplay/Relics.md` in the guide is only empty headings (Compass, Wheel, Telescope, Horn), so effects below for the 3 nav relics come from level gating, not a written description.

## Relics (4)
| Relic | Where obtained | Effect / use |
|---|---|---|
| Compass | Blacksmith | One of 3 relics required to open Dock (ship) |
| Steering Wheel | Lava World ("Wheel Pickup" sub-area; guide notes "wheel unmissable", and a "wheel skip" exists) | Required for Dock |
| Telescope | Industrial Castle | Required for Dock |
| Horn | Corn Boss | Usable item: blows notes; opens horn-sign spots (Hawkster in Tall Grass Field, BiPolar Bear at Ice Castle) |
Dock gate: "Requires at least one player to have the Compass, Steering Wheel and Telescope acquired."

## Usable key items
| Item | Where | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Bow & Arrow | Start (Barbarian War / Forest Entrance) | Ranged, damage scales with Agility |
| Boomerang | Cardinal orb fetch (Industrial Castle) / Thieves Forest path | Pickup at range, opens breakable doors |
| Shovel | Desert | Dig spots (Giraffey, Mr. Buddy, Frogglet, Chicken, Treasure Hunter) |
| Bombs / Potions / Sandwich | Stores (consumables) | Bombs open cracked walls; sandwich = beefy form (boulders, doors) |
| Map | Sand Castle Roof | World map progression |
| Key Sword | Cardinal at Full Moon | Opens Animal Ark door (Beholder) |

## Unobtainable / platform notes
- No relic is DLC- or platform-locked; all four are story items. Nothing to remove.
- Horn is "bugged on PS3 insane mode" (bring from normal) - make sure it carries over on Vita.
- Platform-exclusive *weapons* (pre-order/DLC) are tracked in `weapons.tsv`, not here.
